#Lost & Found System for Colleges

##Problem Statement

Students often lose personal belongings on campus and struggle to find them.

##Proposed Solution

A centralized digital platform to upload, search, and recover lost items within college campuses.

##Key Features

- Upload lost and found items with photos.
- Add item descriptions, locations, and dates.
- Search and filter items by category, place, and date.
- Connect item owners with finders.
- Matching-item notifications (planned).

##Target Users

College students, faculty, and campus administrators.
